The render's layout primitives now measure the columns a terminal draws, so colour is representable at all. `@reddb-io/redskilled-render` emitted zero ANSI not by decision but by construction: `width()` counted one character as one column, so a `k=v` wearing `\x1b[38;2;255;214;214m` measured nineteen columns wider than it drew — the degradation ladder dropped detail nobody was out of room for, `pad()` misaligned every coloured cell against its uncoloured neighbours, and `clamp()` could cut mid-escape and leave the terminal painted for every row that followed. `width()`, `clamp()` and `pad()` now strip SGR before counting; `clamp()` spends its budget on visible columns alone, never splits an escape, and emits a reset when it cuts a line that opened a colour, because a truncation that swallows the terminator is the same defect as one that swallows half of it. The wine identity family moves into one `palette.ts` — lifted verbatim from `statusline-style.ts` rather than re-picked, since two palettes for one product is the drift this package exists to end, and the lifecycle bar's ramp stays on the wine family rather than reverting to the green/yellow traffic light it was deliberately moved off. Colour stays unconditional: nothing in the package reads `NO_COLOR` or probes a TTY, because a renderer that decides for itself is a renderer that reads the world — a caller wanting a plain string calls the newly exported `stripAnsi` at its own boundary. `shortModel` moves in alongside the other pure string work, and `apps/dev` imports it rather than holding a second spelling. Nothing visible changes yet; this is the foundation the rendering work consumes.
