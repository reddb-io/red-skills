Ratchet the live ownership vocabulary and pin the focused ACP CI posture

The Dev glossary retired four ownership nouns — the per-project Castle resident,
the Demand producer, a Project coordinator Worker, and Manager as a service —
into redskilled's Project control state and the operator-facing Manager Skill. A
glossary refuses nothing, so `domain-vocabulary-guard` declares each phrase
beside the live owner that replaced it and sweeps the control-plane trees for it.
History is distinguished rather than tolerated: comments are stripped before
matching, and every surviving literal is declared one site at a time as
`historical` or as `pending-demolition` under the ratchet whose count owns its
removal.

Issue #3878 removed three broad pull-request gates (RSP, the whole
`apps/redskilled` suite, and the engine package's) in favour of focused suites. A
removal nothing pins is a removal that grows back, so `acp-ci-posture` asserts
both directions at once, and a new focused matrix names and probes the five
supported Agents — Redcode, Claude Code, Codex, Pi and OpenCode — at the ACP
baseline before a Worker may reach any of them.
