ADR 0130's amendments are renumbered into one contiguous chronological sequence. Two amendments both carried the number 1 — the activity poller and the host-scoped home — and both were referenced by that number across the tree, so "Amendment 1" named two different decisions depending on which reader you asked. The home becomes Amendment 2, the machine scope Amendment 3, and the demand loop Amendment 4; every reference is repointed by meaning rather than by pattern, since ADR 0041 and ADR 0089 have amendments of their own that a blanket rewrite would have corrupted.
