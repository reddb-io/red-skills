`rsp` parses its arguments through the shared contract instead of a hand-rolled parser (#2873). Every hand-rolled parser has its own answer to the questions a parser must answer — what counts as a flag, what happens to an unknown one, whether `-v` means version or verbose, whether a command can be omitted — and those answers differed per binary. That is why `--version` existed on some shipped binaries and not others: nothing guaranteed it, so each author decided separately. The gap was found by running the binaries rather than reading them, since grepping for the *import* of the version helper suggested a coverage that did not exist.

**Routing and flags now go through `@reddb-io/shared/args`.** `rsp` declares which commands and which flags are its own; `routeCommand` peels the subcommand, `parseFlags` reads the flags of the commands that own their whole argv (`server`, `warm-resident`, `stats`, `gains`, `doctor`, `gh-api-json`), and `--version`/`-v` — with `--json` for structured build info — and `--help`/`-h` are answered from that contract before config, enablement, the store, or the socket is touched. An unknown flag and an unknown subcommand each fail naming the token, rather than defaulting into a surprise. The bare invocation is now named `dashboard` rather than left unrouted, and `rsp dashboard` is its explicit spelling.

The contract itself grew the one shape a wrapper needs and `parseFlags` cannot provide: `extractFlags` peels the declared flags out of an argv that is only partly ours and hands back every other token untouched. `parseFlags` absorbs unknown flags, which is correct for a CLI that owns its whole argv and destructive for one that proxies a foreign tail — under it, `rsp git log --oneline -n 5` would have lost `--oneline` and turned `5` into a positional. A bare `--` ends extraction by default, so a token past the separator that merely shares our spelling is not consumed; the wrapper-side lever scan opts out of that, because there the separator is `git diff -- <path>`'s and rsp appended its own `--query` beyond it.
