# 0136 — One custodian, one verdict, one park

A supervised nine-hour drain (2026-08-05) needed ~30 manual interventions, and an architecture exploration traced nearly all of them to three shallow surfaces: six non-communicating implementations of "make sure this PR is queued" (with the shipped repair lane having zero production callers), five layered classifiers answering "whose fault is this failure" with documented contradictions, and three parsers of the `red:blocker-state` block holding three different definitions of "active" beside three unequal requeue appliers. Each intervention was a human standing in for a missing deep module.

## Decisions

1. **The Queue Custodian owns "this pull request ends merged."** Native auto-merge is the primary intent-holder — a merge must survive a dead resident, which two daemon wedges in one night proved non-negotiable — and the castle merge-driver's loop survives only as the Custodian's recovery arm, woken when the native intent has vanished (the ejection). The two engines are adapters behind one seam; the six ad-hoc re-queue sites become callers.
2. **The daemon watches; a repair Worker heals.** Custody detection lives in the redskilled daemon's periodic sweep (ETag makes re-polls free), and a detected ejection births a repair-lane Worker through ordinary admission — the ADR 0130 shape, finally giving the repair lane its caller.
3. **Landing hands off and ends.** A Worker opens the PR, arms the intent, records the custody hand-off, and terminates; the issue closes when the merge lands. The merge-poll occupation (~40 min of slot per issue) and the `ci-wait` park class cease to exist — the Custodian is the one watcher.
4. **Semantic queue rejection re-queues with evidence, bounded at two.** The Custodian writes the failure evidence and returns the Ticket `ready-for-agent` with branch adoption; the second semantic bounce of the same PR parks `ready-for-human` with the history. The night's manual fix-forward requeues are the machine's own move now.
5. **The Verdict is one pure function, and the classification hook dies.** `(checks, history, environment) → {fault, budget effect, park?}` replaces the five-layer stack whose contradictions #3317 orphaned. `on_feedback_classify` is deleted — its one real case (a suite that legitimately finishes under a second) becomes an operator declaration beside the Validation moment, per the never-guess/declare philosophy. All environment causes draw from **one** capped ledger with **one** repeated-signature fast-park; exhaustion parks as `infra`, never charged to the branch.
6. **The Park has one door.** `blocker-state` deepens into the sole writer/parser/clearer of the block; the requeue transition is a single applier carrying claim-sweep, base-freshness, and the directive on every path, with **authority as a parameter**: machine callers stay restricted to mechanical kinds, human authority passes any kind through the same door. A malformed block (`status: blocked` with missing fields) is **active and named as a defect** — fail closed, with the malformation surfaced for repair instead of each reader deciding alone. The ADR 0122 transition planner becomes the one label vocabulary; the legacy validator and the duplicated constants go.

## Considered options

- **Driver-only merging** — rejected: with a native queue the driver is just a hand-rolled `--auto`, and it couples every release to resident liveness; the night's releases survived two daemon deaths precisely because GitHub held the intent.
- **Workers waiting for their merges (status quo)** — rejected: redundant with custody, burns ~40 min of slot per issue, and its timeout is itself a park class.
- **A symmetric-power classification hook** — rejected: a runtime override inside a truth function is the reentry door for the contradictions being deleted.
- **Separate per-cause environment budgets** — rejected: the split was an accident of two implementations, never a product decision.
